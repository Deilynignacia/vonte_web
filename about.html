Where everything started

“Dance is the hidden language of the soul”

-Martha Graham

Vonté began as a free community project, bringing together 22 passionate performers between the ages of 9 and 18. Our journey officially took the stage when we debuted as a guest company at the XIII Festival Interescolar de Danza Teatro, held at the Teatro Municipal Pompeya and organized by Colegio Boston.

Our mission has always been to make the performing arts approachable for everyday people, breaking down the barriers between the stage and the public. While we have evolved from a free initiative into a formal, self-sustaining company, our commitment remains firmly rooted in keeping theater and dance highly accessible to everyone.

Unlike a traditional academy focused on imparting technique, Vonté operates as a collaborative creative company. We focus on the artistic development of our cast through active project creation, working closely towards professional improvement and expression by bringing complete productions to life.
